## Target Audience

**Who:** Homeowners in Spring City, Royersford, Phoenixville, Pottstown, North Coventry, South Coventry, Birchrunville — Owen J. Roberts school district corridor, Montgomery + Chester Counties PA.

**Decision maker:** The homeowner managing the house — often whoever handles household maintenance decisions.

**Primary use case:** Get something fixed, built, or renovated without having to find, vet, and babysit an unreliable contractor.

**Jobs to be done:**
- Get a specific repair done fast without a runaround
- Find ONE person who can handle multiple things (don't want to call 4 different contractors)
- Trust that the person showing up won't rip them off or leave the job half-done

**Scenarios:**
- Honey-do list that keeps growing
- Getting the house ready to sell
- Just moved in, needs work
- Seasonal: lawn in spring/summer, snow removal in winter
- One project leads to another (handyman relationship)

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Finding a local contractor who actually shows up, does quality work, and doesn't disappear after the deposit.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Big companies send whoever's available — no accountability, no relationship
- Cheap handymen flake, do shoddy work, or never finish
- Nextdoor recommendations are a crapshoot
- Home Depot / Angi leads feel like strangers — no trust
- Getting multiple quotes is exhausting

**What it costs them:**
- Time — chasing contractors, rescheduling, supervising
- Money — paying for shoddy work twice
- Stress — project drags on, house is torn apart

**Emotional tension:**
- "I just want someone I can trust"
- "I don't want a stranger in my house I can't vouch for"
- "I've been burned before"

## Customer Language

**How they describe the problem:**
- "I just need someone reliable"
- "I can't find anyone who actually shows up"
- "I've been waiting 3 weeks for a callback"
- "My husband/wife keeps putting it off"

**How they describe a good contractor:**
